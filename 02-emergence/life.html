The Game of Life

Birth on exactly 3 neighbours, survival on 2 or 3, death otherwise. Nothing in those rules mentions motion, guns or computers. Click or drag on the grid to draw. Newborn cells are tinted.

generation 0population 0

Things to try and wonder about

  • Glider: after 4 generations it has the same shape, moved one cell diagonally. Its speed, c/4, is a "law of physics" that emerges from the rules.
  • R-pentomino: 5 cells, about 1100 generations of chaos. (On this wrapped-around board it may hit its own debris. On an infinite board it settles at generation 1103.) Could you have predicted that without running it? This is computational irreducibility.
  • Glider gun: the population graph becomes a staircase that grows forever, one glider every 30 generations.
  • Random soup: run it many times. Chaos always seems to cool into the same small set of "species". Why?
  • Life is Turing complete, so in general "will this pattern ever die out?" is undecidable. See module 05.
